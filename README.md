One image that sums up Friday 20 November 2026: a dense newspaper front
page, drawn with Python (PIL) and delivered as `entry.png`.

- **The date is the masthead.** "Friday 20 November 2026" is the largest
  text on the page, so the day it sums up cannot be missed.
- **The day in numbers.** Six giant figures anchor the page: 300,000
  evacuated as Cyclone Akvan landed, Anthropic's first close of $97.17,
  Phillips' 118, the Senate's 51–48 Iran vote, Africa's $120bn-a-year
  climate demand, and GTA VI's Metacritic 99.
- **13 illustrated stories.** Three leads with big in-picture labels —
  Cyclone Akvan, Kyiv under fire, COP31's final day without a deal — plus
  ten illustrated medium stories, each with a bold headline and a source.
- **36 stories in all.** A world map with numbered pins for stories 1–13
  and a numbered ALSO ON index (14–36) across every region and beat.
- **Date check.** A red-bordered strip lists what was deliberately left
  out because it was first reported on Thursday 19 November.

Every figure was checked against two or more sources. The program is
deterministic: running `draw.py` twice produces byte-identical PNGs, and
`main.ts` copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`.
